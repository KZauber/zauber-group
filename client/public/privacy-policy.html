Privacy Policy

3 Angels Security DBA Zauber Group

Last Updated: July 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how 3 Angels Security DBA Zauber Group ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects the information you provide through our websites, including zaubergroup.com and grow.zaubergroup.com. By using our websites and submitting your information, you agree to the practices described below.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the personal and business information you voluntarily provide when you submit a form on one of our websites. Depending on which form you use, this may include your name, business or company name, website address, city and state, business type, email address, and phone number, along with any details you choose to share about your business, the program you're interested in, or your goals.

We do not collect payment, financial, or government-identification information through our websites.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Respond to your inquiries, schedule calls, and provide customer support.
  • Prepare and deliver the reports and resources you request, such as a visibility report or a downloadable checklist.
  • Deliver the marketing services you engage us for.
  • Send you marketing emails (such as our nurture sequence and newsletter) where you have opted in by requesting a resource or subscribing. Every marketing email includes an unsubscribe link.
  • Send you transactional SMS messages (appointment reminders, scheduling, support updates, and service confirmations), where you have consented.
  • Send you marketing SMS messages (promotions, special offers, and announcements), only if you separately opt in to them.

3. SMS / Text Messaging

Where we offer SMS, we provide two separate opt-ins, and each one requires its own consent, given by checking the corresponding box on our form:

  • Transactional messages: appointment reminders, scheduling, support updates, and service confirmations.
  • Marketing messages: promotions, special offers, discounts, and announcements.

We do not send you text messages unless you have opted in. By opting in, you agree to receive text messages from 3 Angels Security DBA Zauber Group at the phone number you provide. Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies.

You can opt out at any time: Reply STOP to unsubscribe. Reply HELP for help.

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

4. We Do Not Sell or Share Your Data

We do not sell, rent, or share your phone number or personal information with third parties for their own marketing purposes. No mobile information or SMS opt-in data is shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Your information is used solely for the purposes described in this policy.

5. Service Providers

We use a small number of third-party service providers to operate our websites and communicate with you — including our website host, form-submission handler, email-delivery provider, and customer relationship management (CRM) software. These providers process your information only on our behalf, only as needed to deliver those services, and are not permitted to use it for their own marketing.

6. Cookies, Analytics & Advertising Tools

Our websites use minimal cookies to help them function. Web fonts are loaded from Google, which may receive your IP address as part of delivering them. We use privacy-friendly website analytics to understand aggregate traffic patterns.

We may also use advertising and analytics tools such as the Meta Pixel and the LinkedIn Insight Tag, which set cookies and collect information about your visit so that we can measure website performance and show you relevant ads on those platforms later. You can control this through your browser's cookie settings and through your ad-preference settings on Meta and LinkedIn.

7. Information About Your Business That Is Publicly Available

When you request a visibility report or a marketing audit, we review publicly available information about your business — such as your website, search-engine listings, business profiles, public reviews, and social media accounts — in order to prepare that report for you. We only review information that is already publicly accessible.

8. Data Removal & Your Rights

You may request that we access, correct, or delete the personal information you have submitted at any time. To do so, contact us by email or phone using the details below and we will take care of your request. You may also unsubscribe from any marketing email using the link in its footer, or opt out of SMS messages at any time by replying STOP.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to keep your information safe.

10. Children's Privacy

Our websites and services are intended for business owners and are not directed to anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last Updated" date at the top reflects the most recent revision.
